## License

- The Macon County Mo ARC VE Team will be offering Ham Radio F.C.C.
License exams for Technician, General, and Extra class levels testing at all future meetings each month  @ 7pm PLEASE RSVP to KF0GUS@hotmail.com in Advance to ensure we have enough VE's available for testing.  

Testing ONLY location is<br>
806 E. Briggs Dr.<br>
Macon, Missouri 65332<br>

1. Please bring Valid Government issued photo ID

2. FRN Number from the F.C.C. cores website you registered for.

[FCC CORES FRN WEB LINK](http://https//apps.fcc.gov/cores/html/Register_New_FRN.htm)

3. $15 ARRL testing fee cash or check ONLY.
4. IF you are upgrading please bring a official copy of your current F.C.C Ham Radio License
5. A valid working email address is required to pay your $35 new License fee or for renewal....NO EXCEPTIONS DIRECTLY and electronically to the F.C.C. website
 [FCC PAYMENT WEB LINK](https://www.fcc.gov/licensing-databases/fees/cores-payment-system)
